02 / Lettering

Two typefaces,
used well.

DM Serif Display for headings, with one phrase in italic as the accent. DM Sans for everything else.

Body text is DM Sans at 16 pixels with plenty of room between lines. Sentences are short. Paragraphs are short. We write plain UK English and say what people get, in their words.
