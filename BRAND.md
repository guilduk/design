# The Guild UK name and marks

The code in this repository is free to use under the MIT licence. **The Guild UK name and marks are not.** They belong to Guild UK Systems Limited and are here so Guild UK products, and products we make with partners, look like one family.

The marks are:

- the name "Guild UK" and the GUILD UK lettering
- the crown, alone or in its frame (`assets/brand/`)
- the octagonal stamp frame when used as a logo or app icon (`assets/icons/`)

## You may

- Use the colours, lettering, spacing and parts in your own project, under your own name.
- Say your project "uses Guild Design" or "is built with Guild Design" and link here.
- Show the marks when writing about Guild UK or its products, unchanged.

## Please do not

- Use the Guild UK name, the crown or the stamp frame as the logo or icon of your own product.
- Say or suggest your product is made, checked or endorsed by Guild UK when it is not.
- Change the marks: recolour, stretch, add effects, or take the crown out of its frame.

If you forked a Guild UK product, change its name and icon before you publish your version.

To ask about using the marks: hello@guilduk.com.
