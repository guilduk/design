# Changes

## 1.1.1, 2 November 2026

- Email dark mode: the main button stays forest with white words (a thin light-green edge in dark), because the Outlook phone apps recolour emails and turned the coral button brick red.
- Email pictures: the logo is white on a see-through background, and the signature mark sits on its own forest tile, so no baked-in box shows when a mail app recolours the email.

## 1.1.0, 2 November 2026

- Email: the same layout as guilduk.com's emails. Logo white on the forest band, white letter, signature with the mark, deep forest footer.
- Email in light and dark: the dark tokens apply in Apple Mail, the Outlook apps and Outlook.com, and the main button turns coral with ink words, as on dark bands on the web.

## 1.0.0, 1 November 2026

- First version: the guilduk.com look as a kit for every Guild UK product.
- Tokens for colour, lettering, space, shape and motion, with light and dark.
- Parts: buttons, links, cards, panels, steps, stats, forms, badges, notices, the stamp, toasts, empty states, tables, app shell, public menu, dialogs.
- Tailwind preset and shadcn/ui colours for Next.js projects.
- Product icons in the stamp frame, with here and health as examples.
- Email template, rules for AI assistants, and a guide to publishing products safely.
